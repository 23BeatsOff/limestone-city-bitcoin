Limestone City Bitcoin

We are a Bitcoin-only group in Kingston, Ontario, focused on education, relationships and building the local community. Anyone can stack and wait. We would rather build.

Whether you are a curious beginner or a seasoned cypherpunk, this group is for you. We talk about:

  • Sound money and economics
  • Wallets and holding your own keys
  • Privacy and freedom technology
  • Nodes, mining and the protocol
  • Building a local Bitcoin community
